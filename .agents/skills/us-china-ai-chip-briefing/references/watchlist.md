# Standing research domains

These domains are research prompts, not required news categories or factual assertions. Rank developments by materiality; an empty domain does not need filler. Case facts live in active_cases.md; historical supplied facts are archived separately and remain unverified.

- US-China AI competition: major model capabilities, open weights, inference efficiency, benchmark methods, actual availability and adoption.
- Semiconductors and compute: accelerators, memory, advanced packaging, manufacturing capacity/yield, software ecosystem, supply chains and cost/performance constraints.
- Policy and enforcement on both sides: export/import controls, licenses, trade arrangements, enforcement, list changes and actual legal effects. Use original official instruments and applicable versions.
- Companies and financing: material deals, issuance, patent/industrial partnerships, contested actions and dated company responses.
- Management implications: underwriting, lending, investment, diligence and reputation where supported; do not invent links or rely on internal bank data.

For any relevant named entity, record current status using exact legal names/aliases, list version and search date. Lack of news is not evidence of unchanged status. Preserve major technical developments even when no direct bank-risk tie exists.
